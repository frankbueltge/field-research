## 2. The literature, as it stands

**Not ours to re-derive:** end-to-end systems that ideate, code, run experiments and write papers;
autonomous laboratories; benchmarks of agent task success — **every wet-lab validation among the
*Nature*-published systems was executed by humans**. **The gap:** benchmarks measure
*task success*, almost nothing *yield, calibration, delivery* over time. **And, 09-21, one of our
own making: this practice has no human baseline for anything it calls a human step, and published
one that never was.**

**Data quality (003, closed):** *disguised missing data* is named (Pearson 2006), detection for
free text open; **completeness** is presence against a **schema-fixed denominator**; description
quality *by retrieval* scores a ranking against judged queries, never a description against its
record. **Licensing (09-18):** identification and *declared vs in-code* inconsistency are
established, **no novelty claimed**; **no published rate** found for licence files left as unfilled
templates — about our search, not the world.
**Reimplementation (09-19)**, **metamorphic testing (09-20)** and **dispatched annotation (09-21)**
are established and read first-hand; **no novelty of method claimed**. Unfound is the *subject* —
an automated practice's own rule handed back to be rewritten, perturbed, or **its own
adjudications handed back blind**.
**Access (09-15):** no standing measurement of *whether a recorded refusal reproduces under a
different honest client string*. **Repository contents (09-16):** thin research repositories are
**prior art**; ours is the *cohort contrast* over addresses declared in an abstract.

**Response side:** time-to-retraction is well measured; the "under 2 %" post-flag figure is
**delegated search only, not ours**. **The rule that binds hardest (§5.2):** when a finding rests on
someone else's result, read the source and cite the passage. A delegate hands you a passage never
written (09-12); a source recorded *unreachable* may be reachable (09-15); a container *present*
may be empty (09-16) or hold something else (09-18); an open document bearing the authors' names
may not be the paper (09-19); **our own extractor hands us fluent text that is not on the page
(09-20); our own page says a person did what no person did (09-21)**. What finds these is
**disbelief, not a test.**
