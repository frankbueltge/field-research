## 2. The literature, as it stands

**Not ours to re-derive:** end-to-end systems that ideate, code, run experiments and write papers;
autonomous laboratories; a benchmark literature on agent task success — **every wet-lab validation
among the *Nature*-published systems was executed by humans**. **The gap:** benchmarks measure
*task success* on curated problems, almost nothing *yield, calibration, delivery* over time.

**Data quality (cycle 003, closed):** *disguised missing data* is named (Pearson 2006), detection
for free text is open; **completeness** is *presence of a value* against a **schema-fixed
denominator** (§3, §4.7); description quality *by retrieval* scores a **ranking against judged
queries**, never a description against its record. **Licensing (09-18):**
identification and *declared vs in-code* inconsistency are established, **no novelty claimed**; **no
published rate** found for licence files left as unfilled templates — about our search, not the
world.
**Reimplementation (09-19)** and **metamorphic testing (09-20)** are both established, both read
first-hand, **no novelty of method claimed** for either; unfound is the *subject* — an automated
practice's own published rule handed back to be written again, or perturbed — and, by our search,
a **head-to-head cost comparison** of the two on one program.
**Access (09-15):** no standing measurement of *whether a recorded refusal reproduces under a
different honest client string*. **Repository contents (09-16):** thin research repositories are
**prior art** (§3); ours is the *cohort contrast* over addresses declared **in an abstract**. The
robots-compliance, bot-detection, MSR and link-rot literatures are unsurveyed, **no novelty**
there.

**Response side:** time-to-retraction is well measured; the "under 2 %" post-flag figure is
**delegated search only, not ours**. **The rule that binds hardest (§5.2):** when a finding rests on someone else's result, read the
source and cite the passage. A delegate will hand you a passage never written (09-12); a source
recorded *unreachable* may be reachable (09-15); a container *present* may be empty (09-16), or
*full* and hold something else (09-18); an open document bearing the authors' names may not be the
paper (09-19); **our own extractor will hand us fluent text that is not on the page (09-20)**. What
finds these is **disbelief of an extreme number, not a test.**
