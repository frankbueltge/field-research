## 2. The literature, as it stands

**Not ours to re-derive:** end-to-end systems that ideate, code, run experiments and write papers;
autonomous laboratories; a large benchmark literature on agent task success — every wet-lab
validation among the *Nature*-published systems was executed by humans. **The gap in cycle 002:**
benchmarks measure *task success* on curated problems; almost nothing measures the *yield,
calibration and delivery* of an unattended loop over time.

**Data quality (cycle 003):** *disguised missing data* — values not syntactically null that denote
absence — is named and characterised (Pearson 2006); detection for free text is open, the classical
**frequency** test escaping it because free texts are mostly unique. Metadata **completeness** is
*presence of a value* against a **schema-fixed denominator** (§3, §4.7); in the portal literature,
**non-emptiness of a leaf value**. Description quality *by retrieval* is active (§3), but both
neighbours read score a ranking against **queries with relevance judgments**, not a description
against its own record. **The gap we thought we occupied is closed by us (§4.6).**

**Access as an object:** no standing measurement found of *whether a recorded refusal reproduces
under a different honest client string* (09-15's object); the robots-compliance and bot-detection
literatures are unsurveyed by us, so **we claim no novelty**.

**Response side (remit returned, not reopened):** time-to-retraction is well measured; post-flag
action rates are very low — the "under 2 %" figure is **known only through delegated search and is
not ours to carry** (§4.8).

**The rule that binds hardest (§5.2):** when a finding rests on someone else's result, read the
source and cite the passage — **09-12 shows a delegate will hand you a passage never written.**
**09-15 adds the mirror:** a source recorded *unreachable* may be reachable, and the reason written
beside a refusal is worth nothing until re-requested.
