## 2. The literature, as it stands

**Not ours to re-derive:** end-to-end systems that ideate, code, run experiments and write papers;
autonomous laboratories; a benchmark literature on agent task success — every wet-lab validation
among the *Nature*-published systems was executed by humans. **The gap:** benchmarks measure *task
success* on curated problems; almost nothing measures *yield, calibration, delivery* over time.

**Data quality (cycle 003):** *disguised missing data* is named (Pearson 2006); detection for free
text is open. **Completeness** is *presence of a value* against a **schema-fixed denominator**
(§3, §4.7). Description quality *by retrieval* is active (§3), but both neighbours score a ranking
against **queries with relevance judgments**, not a description against its record.

**Licensing (09-18):** licence identification and *declared vs in-code* inconsistency are an
established literature; **no novelty of method claimed**. **No published rate** found for licence
files left as unfilled templates — a statement about our search, not the world.
**Reimplementation (09-19):** N-version programming and the multiple-analyst studies are
established and **no novelty of method is claimed**; unfound is the *subject* — an automated
practice's own published rule handed back to be written again.
**Access (09-15):** no standing measurement of *whether a recorded refusal reproduces under a
different honest client string*. **Repository contents (09-16):** thin research repositories are **prior art** (§3); our claim is
the *cohort contrast* over addresses declared **in an abstract**. The robots-compliance,
bot-detection, mining-software-repositories and link-rot literatures are unsurveyed, so **no
novelty** there.

**Response side:** time-to-retraction is well measured; the "under 2 %" post-flag figure is
**delegated search only, not ours**.

**The rule that binds hardest (§5.2):** when a finding rests on someone else's result, read the
source and cite the passage. **09-12:** a delegate will hand you a passage never written.
**09-15:** a source recorded *unreachable* may be reachable. **09-16:** a container recorded
*present* may be empty. **09-18:** one *present and full* may hold something else. **09-19:** an
open document bearing the authors' names may not be the paper — and what finds these is
**disbelief of an extreme number, not a test.**
