## 2. The literature, as it stands

**Not ours to re-derive:** end-to-end systems that ideate, code, run experiments and write papers;
autonomous laboratories; a large benchmark literature on agent task success — every wet-lab
validation among the *Nature*-published systems was executed by humans. **The gap:** benchmarks
measure *task success* on curated problems; almost nothing measures *yield, calibration, delivery*
over time.

**Data quality (cycle 003):** *disguised missing data* is named (Pearson 2006); detection for free
text is open. Metadata **completeness** is *presence of a value* against a **schema-fixed
denominator** (§3, §4.7); in the portal literature, **non-emptiness of a leaf value**. Description
quality *by retrieval* is active (§3), but both neighbours score a ranking against **queries with
relevance judgments**, not a description against its record.

**Licensing (09-18):** licence identification and *declared vs in-code* inconsistency are an
established literature; **no novelty of method claimed**. We found **no published rate** for licence
files left as unfilled templates — a statement about our search, not the world.
**Access (09-15):** no standing measurement of *whether a recorded refusal reproduces under a
different honest client string*. **Repository contents (09-16):** that research repositories are
thin is **prior art** (§3); our claim is the *cohort contrast* on a model-free ladder over addresses
declared **in an abstract**. The robots-compliance, bot-detection, mining-software-repositories and
link-rot literatures are unsurveyed by us, so **no novelty** there either.

**Response side:** time-to-retraction is well measured; post-flag action rates are very low — the
"under 2 %" figure is **delegated search only, not ours to carry**.

**The rule that binds hardest (§5.2):** when a finding rests on someone else's result, read the
source and cite the passage. **09-12:** a delegate will hand you a passage never written.
**09-15:** a source recorded *unreachable* may be reachable. **09-16:** a container recorded
*present* may be empty. **09-18:** one recorded *present and full* may hold something else — and
**what finds that is disbelief of a clean number, not a test.**
