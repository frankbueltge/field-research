### Cycle 002 (2026-09-03 – 2026-09-07) — closed, `presentations/cycle-002/`

Built a research loop that runs unattended (seven stages, ~90 s, nightly) and **everything came from
it failing, in four ways.** (1) The **mechanical middle automates cleanly and is calibrated** —
per-test rejection 4.88 % (CI 4.66–5.12) in 500 permuted worlds; the loop manufactures findings
because it asks 66 questions and for no other reason, and **null yield is linear in k** while
**redundancy is statistically inert**. (2) Automation moves the failure **upstream into the question
space**, invisible from inside. (3) The step that resists is **recognising that what you hold is what
the world already has**: PRIOR-ART retrieves **0 of 9** known targets from prose that never names
them, **3 of 9** from the bare name. (4) **An unattended loop needs an instrument for its own
liveness**: three nights ran green measuring nothing. **Tarone 1990** (§3) excludes hypotheses that
cannot reach α. **Thirty defects taken off four artifacts by convened adversaries.**

## 2. The literature, as it stands

**Not ours to re-derive:** end-to-end systems that ideate, code, run experiments and write papers;
autonomous laboratories; a large benchmark literature on agent task success. Every wet-lab validation
among the *Nature*-published systems was executed by humans. **The standing gap we occupied in cycle
002:** benchmarks measure *task success* on curated problems; almost nothing measures the *yield,
calibration and delivery* of a research loop running unattended over time.

**Data quality (cycle 003):** *disguised missing data* — values not syntactically null that denote
absence — is named and characterised (Pearson 2006); detection for free text is open, and the
classical **frequency** test escapes it because free texts are mostly unique. Metadata
**completeness** is *presence of a value* against a **schema-fixed denominator** (§3, §4.7); in the
portal literature, **non-emptiness of a leaf value**.
Description quality *by retrieval* exists and is active (§3, 2026-09-12), but both neighbours read
score a ranking against **queries with relevance judgments**, not a description against its own
record. **The gap we thought we occupied is now closed by us, not by them (§4.6):** duplicate
detection is prior art, and no operationalisation of "unusable" we can build reproduces itself.

**Response side (remit returned, not yet reopened):** time-to-retraction is well measured; post-flag
action rates are very low — the "under 2 %" figure is **known here only through delegated search and
is not ours to carry** (see §4.8 on what delegated reading did this cycle).

**The rule that binds hardest (§5.2):** when a finding rests on someone else's result, read the
source and cite the passage — and **2026-09-12 shows a delegate will hand you a passage that was
never written.**
