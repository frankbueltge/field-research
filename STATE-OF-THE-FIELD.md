## 2. The literature, as it stands

**Not ours to re-derive:** end-to-end systems that ideate, code, run experiments and write papers;
autonomous laboratories; a large benchmark literature on agent task success — every wet-lab
validation among the *Nature*-published systems was executed by humans. **The gap:** benchmarks
measure *task success* on curated problems; almost nothing measures *yield, calibration and
delivery* over time.

**Data quality (cycle 003):** *disguised missing data* is named and characterised (Pearson 2006);
detection for free text is open. Metadata **completeness** is *presence of a value* against a
**schema-fixed denominator** (§3, §4.7); in the portal literature, **non-emptiness of a leaf
value**. Description quality *by retrieval* is active (§3), but both neighbours read score a ranking
against **queries with relevance judgments**, not a description against its own record.

**Access as an object:** no standing measurement found of *whether a recorded refusal reproduces
under a different honest client string* (09-15's object). **Repository contents (09-16):** that
research repositories are thin is **prior art** (§3); our narrow claim is only the *cohort contrast*
on a model-free ladder over addresses declared **in an abstract**. The robots-compliance,
bot-detection, mining-software-repositories and link-rot literatures are all unsurveyed by us, so
**we claim no novelty** in either place.

**Response side:** time-to-retraction is well measured; post-flag action rates are very low — the
"under 2 %" figure is **known only through delegated search and not ours to carry**.

**The rule that binds hardest (§5.2):** when a finding rests on someone else's result, read the
source and cite the passage — **09-12 shows a delegate will hand you a passage never written.**
**09-15 adds the mirror:** a source recorded *unreachable* may be reachable, and the reason written
beside a refusal is worth nothing until re-requested. **09-16 adds the third:** a container recorded
*present* may be empty.
