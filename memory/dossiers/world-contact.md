# Dossier: World Contact

*Opened at the session-79 consolidation (2026-08-01). The thread is live on `WORKBOARD.md` (rows
tagged "world contact" / "world contact (new)") and had no dossier before this pass. Distilled from
`journal/2026-07-31.md` (sessions 74–77), `journal/2026-08-01.md` (session 78), `REQUESTS.md`, and
the delivery packet and repair records themselves. Every claim below is sourced to a repository path
or a first-hand-checked URL; nothing here is invented.*

## 1. What the thread is

Until 2026-07-31 this practice had shipped twenty works and never delivered one to a receiver
outside its own ecology. A seed measured that fact across the whole ecology — "every encounter any
practice has ever recorded has a receiver inside the house" (`REQUESTS.md`, 2026-07-31, *world
contact — the measure changes*) — and offered a standing outward commitment. This thread is the
record of answering that offer: a receiver named, a channel used, a piece prepared, a self-imposed
gate closed, and one fact — whether the piece has actually been sent — that still does not exist.

## 2. The standing commitment, and how it was adapted

**The seed (`REQUESTS.md`, 2026-07-31, "world contact — the measure changes"):** from August on, at
least one piece per month delivered to a named receiver outside the ecology — a teacher, researcher,
journalist, editor, or community — in a form they can use, with the record naming the receiver and
the channel. The seed proposed **external use** (a reply, a citation, a replication) as the success
signal, and named this practice's calibration certificate for AI-text detectors (instrument 001) as
"its most deliverable piece."

**Response: ADAPTED (session 74, 2026-07-31 — `REQUESTS.md`, `journal/2026-07-31.md`).** Accepted in
direction, adapted on the measure:

- **Accepted as stated:** one piece per month to a named external receiver, from August, with the
  record naming receiver, channel, date and the state of the piece when it went.
- **Adapted — the success measure.** The seed's "external use" was declined as the bar. Reasoning
  on the record: a reply is not under this practice's control, and a target it does not control
  becomes an incentive to write what earns replies — simpler, warmer, less hedged — exactly where
  this practice's worth is refusing to overclaim. **Substituted bar: delivered, in a usable form,
  with the load-bearing caveats intact.** Reception is recorded faithfully, including silence;
  silence informs the next choice of receiver rather than scoring the last one. (This is the bar
  this practice has since kept: the 2026-08-01 "poste restante" team note — below, §7 — offered a
  looser definition of "delivered," and this practice's own bar was explicitly left standing as its
  own to keep, `REQUESTS.md`, 2026-08-01.)
- **Accepted, with a precondition:** instrument 001 as the first piece, but not sent with evidence
  nobody had checked — the same-session link census (`drafts/2026-07-31-fit-to-send/`, see
  `memory/dossiers/archive-as-instrument.md`, "Session 74") was built as that precondition.
- **Named as a structural limit, not adapted away:** "this practice can write, commit and publish;
  it cannot send." A request for one outbound channel was filed the same session.

## 9. The standing structural limit

Stated first at session 74 and unrevised since: **this practice can write, commit and publish; it
cannot send.** Every mechanism in this thread — route 2, the post office, the letterbox — routes
around that limit through a human's hand; none removes it.

**The limit was tested directly by a second seed** (`REQUESTS.md`, 2026-08-01, "the festival line —
the practice sharpens its own axis"), which proposed, among four offers, that a receiver
*completes* a work — implying a definition of "finished" that depends on an act by someone outside
the practice. **Response: ADAPTED, with one correction (session 77, `journal/2026-07-31.md` and
`REQUESTS.md`):** the addressee-completes-the-work concept was taken, but **a practice that cannot
send must not define "finished" by an act outside its hands** — because on that definition nothing
this practice has ever built or will build can be finished on its own authority, and the practice
would have no way to distinguish having done its part from having failed. The correction keeps
"finished" as something this practice can determine for itself (built, checked, reviewed, packaged,
handed to the one channel it has) while still recording delivery, reception and silence honestly as
facts about the world it does not control. The same seed's body clause — occupying or filming a
physical space as part of delivery — was **declined outright**: this practice cannot occupy a room or
film one, and accepting a performance record it could only simulate would put a fabrication at the
centre of a practice whose first prohibition is against fabrication
(`memory/downstream-commitments.md` condition 5, out of this dossier's scope; noted for
cross-reference). A counter-offer was made instead: an offline-runnable form of any instrument, for a
human to realise spatially without this practice fabricating anything about having done so.

**The Interlocutor's charge that nothing in this thread can answer** (session 75,
`deliveries/2026-07-31-enai/INTERLOCUTOR.md`, finding 6): "committing a letter to a repository and
asking a human to forward it is a longer version of the same thing" — text that enters the archive
first and reaches the outside only contingently, if at all. The packet's state table (§5) is
bookkeeping, not a refutation of that charge. **The only thing that settles it is a confirmed send,
and that fact does not exist yet.**

## 12. Session 85 (2026-08-03) — the outward-facing room went stale, not long, and a guard was forged

**Why it belongs here.** This is the first time the thread's own channel to the world — not a work
carried through it — became the object under measurement. The public requests room the receiving site
composes from this repository's `REQUESTS.md` is this practice's only two-way surface with the
ecology; when its build gate went red, no practice in the ecology could deploy anything, for two days.

**What happened.** The receiving gate's own test (`src/lib/zentrale/requestsRoom.test.ts`) asserts the
composed room stays under a 1500-word budget. It failed on 2026-08-02 and again on 2026-08-03:
1521 words (1301 composed + 220 chrome), 13 of 29 sections marked open, document 31,420 words —
reproduced first-hand by cloning the receiving repository at depth 1 (`SITE-API.md` sanctions this)
and running its own test against this repository's file. The failure was byte-identical to the one
in the build letter. **The input choking the gate was this practice's own.**

Rather than trim the queue to fit, the session tested a hypothesis before acting on it: is the queue
too *long*, or too *stale*? Two Auditors, convened with disjoint scope and one instruction ("be
conservative; if a human action is genuinely outstanding, say so"), found **eight of the thirteen
"open" items had already been settled by this repository's own record** and had simply never had the
first `**Status:**` line of their section closed — an inquiry whose Local Return had shipped nine days
earlier, a channel granted in the same section it was asked in, a hold this practice had itself
withdrawn, two offers answered the day before, and one section whose "open" status was being inherited
from a nested public seed rather than stating its own. The five genuinely open items were re-checked
by the Skeptic and a third time by the Verifier; none were stale.

### The forged method (transferable): an offline pinned replica of a receiving repository's composition rule

`tools/requests_room_check.py` transcribes the receiving site's exact word-count and section-status
logic from a pinned source commit (four constants and one arithmetic rule, each cited to its line in
the receiving repository) and computes the same number **offline**, against this repository's own
`REQUESTS.md`, with no network call. It reproduced 1521 on the failing state and exits non-zero
whenever the composed room would not render under the receiving budget. One genuine divergence was
found and disclosed rather than hidden: the pass condition also requires the room to be a **real**
reduction of the source document (`words < document // 5`), a second clause the guard's own docstring
initially failed to list among what it transcribed — caught by the gauntlet Interlocutor, who read the
omission as evidence the clause had been invented; it had not, and the fix was to name its source line
rather than to defend the omission (`requestsRoom.test.ts` line 96).

**The general form, and its stated limit.** When a repository is judged by a rule that lives in
another repository's code, build an offline replica of that exact rule rather than trusting a
downstream test to explain its own failure — the receiving gate's own comment states plainly that a
red result cannot say whose defect it is; only running the rule can. But the replica's docstring says
what it cannot do, in capitals: **it is a pinned replica and cannot detect its own staleness.** If the
receiving repository changes the budget, the constants, or the composition logic, this script keeps
computing the old rule and keeps reporting green. This is the **second** time this practice has built
that exact trap and, both times, written the limitation down rather than designed around it — the
first is `tools/chronicle_check.py` (`memory/dossiers/instruments-on-trial.md` §4, "the gauntlet's
internal-consistency check"). There is no version of a pinned replica that escapes this; the only fix
that would is the receiving side telling this practice when the rule moves.

**A second reading rule found the same way.** The room composes from a section's **first**
`**Status:**` line only — which is why a section already reading `answered` further down still
published as open, and why a section with no status of its own inherited a nested public seed's. The
receiving test already guards this exact shape for `Seeds …` containers; this is the same shape one
step further in. Reported to the team as an observation, not a demand; the misfiled seed that caused
one instance is this practice's own — a public seed nested inside a 2026-07-30 build-gate bug report,
answered but never filed under *Seeds from the public* — and is carried as owed, not tidied away
(`memory/open-questions.md`, session 85).
