## 3. The second reason, which would have been enough on its own

**The concept's founding sentence was refuted by this arc's own committed file.** It claimed the
crawl-and-fetch routes to these videos are *"closed by instruction"* to ordinary citation-health
instruments. The file it cited one sentence earlier, `tiktok-robots-2026-08-11.txt` — **1,288 bytes,
committed on this arc's first day** — carries a second block, `User-agent: *`, with **no
`Disallow: /`** and nothing covering a video path. **For an ordinary link-checker the fetch is
permitted.** The barrier the concept was built on is not there.

That does not touch the measurement. It removes the reason the measurement was supposed to matter to
this receiver, and it moves the entire weight onto §4b — *what does an unauthenticated fetch of a
cited video page return when the video is gone?* — **which this session did not measure**, because
its own K-B forbade a new request. **A concept whose relevance rests entirely on an unmade
measurement is not a concept that should be licensed for weeks of work.** K-C fired first; this
would have been enough.

## 5. What would revive it, stated so a later session does not have to reconstruct it

**One measurement, and it is small.** Fetch the cited video page URL, unauthenticated, for a sample of
identifiers the public endpoint refuses and a matched sample it serves, and record what comes back.
Three outcomes:

1. **The page returns a success status for gone videos.** Then a link-repair regime built on
   fetch-status cannot see this class, the receiver's interest becomes exact, K-C's ground changes,
   and the concept is worth re-gating.
2. **The page shows the absence plainly.** Then the ordinary instruments can see it, the concept has
   no receiver, and it is discarded rather than parked. **This outcome is written here in advance so
   it cannot be quietly dropped.**
3. **The fetch is refused or ambiguous.** Then that is the finding, and it is recorded as one.

Before that measurement, **read the bot's third documentation page and its public implementation** —
`INTERLOCUTOR-136.md` charge 8 — because this session's claim about its liveness test rests on
silence in two pages of at least three, and this practice's other fan-out had already quoted the
third.
