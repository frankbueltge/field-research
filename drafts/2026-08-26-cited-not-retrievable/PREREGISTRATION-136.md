## 3. Question B — whether a new arc may be opened, and under what kill criteria

**The proposal.** A concept gate for a new arc built on the instrument that survives the stopped one
(`POST-MORTEM.md` §6: *"the instrument keeps running… the tooling is real and portable"*), on a
question the stopped arc never asked and a receiver it never had.

**The objection to be met first, and it is the one that decides.** `CONDITIONS-128.md`'s stop says
*"no packet from **this arc**"*. A new arc on the same instrument, opened three days before
`D_guaranteed`, is exactly what a practice wanting to route around its own stop would open, and the
distinction between *this arc* and *a new arc* is one this practice would be drawing in its own
favour. **It is therefore not this session's to settle.** It goes to the adversary as its blocking
charge, and the criteria below fire on the adversary's answer, not on this session's.

**Kill criteria, locked now. Any one of them fires and the gate FAILS**, and a failed gate means
park or discard with a one-page finding — not a rewrite until it passes.

| # | fires if |
|---|---|
| **K-A** | The neighbours search finds a published, running or completed study measuring the retrievability of platform-video citations in this corpus, or a comparable citation corpus, **with per-item re-request confirmation**. The daylight must be named, not asserted. |
| **K-B** | The first checkable increment cannot be computed **today, from files already in this repository** — no new corpus, no new fetch, no new infrastructure. An arc that needs building before it can show anything may not be gated ten days before the reading. |
| **K-C** | No real, reachable receiver outside the house can be named with a **published** interest in this exact question. A receiver invented to satisfy the gate is a fabrication and is treated as one. |
| **K-D** | The object would need any file frozen under `verify_freeze.sh`, or any text from the letter, or a tenth gauntlet on either. This is a new object or it is nothing. |
| **K-E** | **The adversary establishes that the new arc is the stopped arc under another name.** Then the gate fails and the stop stands whole. |

**And one thing that is not a kill criterion, stated so it cannot be smuggled in later:** that the
reading is ten days away is **not** an argument for the gate. If the gate passes, it passes on the
question, and if the licensed arc cannot get an object through a gauntlet in time, then it does not,
and condition 1 fails as `POST-MORTEM.md` §7 already said it would. **No decision in this session may
rest on the calendar alone** — the constraint `PREREGISTRATION-135.md` §4 wrote and this session
adopts unchanged.
