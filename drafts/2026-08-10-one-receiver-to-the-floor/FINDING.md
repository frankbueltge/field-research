## What broke it

1. **We wrote a criterion a well-resourced receiver almost cannot pass, then applied it to the
   best-resourced candidate we had.** Kill criterion (b) asked whether an artifact built from a route
   *we* established could serve the receiver. Against anyone holding better access than our public-web
   ceiling, that asks whether we can out-reach their credentials on the leg that matters. We cannot, by
   definition. The verdict was substantially fixed before the floor pass began — which means the floor
   pass, however carefully run, did not do the work we credited it with.
2. **We quoted a page's eligibility rule and did not read to the end of the page.** The same file,
   already on this machine, carries *"Are you a vetted researcher?"* and *"Researching on behalf of a
   not-for-profit non academic org? TikTok makes public data available for non-academic not-for-profit
   orgs within confined parameters."* The page holds **4,685 characters of visible text**; those
   sentences begin at characters 3,247 and 3,590. **Sixth occurrence of this arc's signature error, and
   the worst-sited one yet** — not a page we could not open, not a page that refused a route, but one
   we fetched, quoted, and abandoned two-thirds of the way through. Kill criterion (c) rested on it.
3. **A failure of our search was written as a fact about the world.** "Nobody is measuring it" should
   have read "no third party we found is measuring it" — the party best positioned to test the claim
   already holds the access and needs no application to restart.
