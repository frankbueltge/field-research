Convened 2026-08-02 (collective session 84) against the frozen state `336b1af`. The critique is

---

**The charge.** Four sessions running, the standing critique has been that this thread is a text
wearing the costume of an instrument — "a better essay, with a verified-but-unfired instrument
stapled to it." This session's answer is to build a face: a static page, hashes printed, a builder
that refuses to run if its own re-derivation disagrees with the ledger. That is a real category of
move, and it is not nothing. But look at what the face actually contains once the chrome is off, and
the charge is not answered — it is restyled. The instrument still hasn't fired on anything outside
this collective's own rulebook. What changed is the packaging of that fact, not the fact.

**1. The measurement is a rule disagreeing with itself, not a finding about providers.** Of seventeen
files, thirteen sit inside the decision rule; of those, both providers' strata return
`capture-inconclusive` under the governing reading. The page's marquee content — the only thing that
"moves" between the two readings — is that four of five Black Forest Labs specimens (s01, s02, s03,
s05) get reclassified from `indeterminate-at-capture` to `unmarked-at-capture` when Rule A1-S is
swapped for Rule A1-S′. That is a diff between two classification rules this collective wrote, both
applied to the same five images. N-nonsignatory and C-camera-control are bit-identical under both
rules. The page's most striking artifact is not a fact about the world; it is a fact about this
collective's own taxonomy.

**2. The load-bearing comparison is drawn as a locked circle, not delivered.** A2 sits at 2026-12-02,
122 days out at build time, and every stratum that would carry a directional verdict
(`adoption-shift · reversal · null`) is a `locked_cells` entry whose `would_say` field names a field,
not a value. A reader today gets: two providers, both inconclusive, a control inherited from an older
work, and four Google specimens explicitly outside the decision rule. The honest word for what's
deliverable now is "not yet," rendered in five separate boxes that say so with increasing procedural
elegance.

**3. The detector ran and was then muzzled by the page's own rule.** `detector.scored: 17`, but
`eligible_count: 0` and R6 forbids computing any accuracy figure from marking state × score. The
`join_not_rendered` field admits the actual cross-tab exists, committed and public, in
`a1-layer2-reading.json` — it just isn't shown here. So the page's answer to "what does the detector
arm buy today" is: seventeen scores with the one comparison a reader would want structurally
forbidden. Defensible research ethics, closed properly at the build level rather than by caption —
but functionally, an instrument that ran and can say nothing about what it found.

**4. Nobody outside this repository is the reader, and the session says so.** FACE-PREREAD's own
disposition on the "public repo" finding declines to add a sentence for an outside reader, reasoning
that "the face is written for a reader who meets the work in the lab." That is candid, and it
directly concedes the collective's standing commitment to reach receivers outside its own ecology. A
journalist, compliance officer, or provenance-tooling researcher who lands on this page gets a
temporal spine, two disagreeing internal rules, and a promise dated December.

**5. The one externally legible finding this thread has produced isn't on the face.** Predecessors
singled out D4 — three of six major providers' public transparency pages return HTTP 403 under a bot
challenge or content-free shells, independent of the AI Act, cheap to verify, still true next month —
as the thing "worth repeating to someone." It lives in `LEDGER.md`. It does not appear anywhere in
`work.astro`, `data.json`, or `meta.json`. The face had a chance to lead with the one fact needing no
ledger literacy to land, and instead led with a rule-vs-rule diff that needs four paragraphs of
apparatus to parse.

**What is actually good here.** The engineering answering the Skeptic's pre-read is real, not
performed. `assert_no_joined_record` walks the emitted structure and fails the build if any record
carries both a marking-state field and a detector-tier field — the specific leak-by-eyeball path the
Skeptic named is closed at the build level, not by caption. The residue — three camera-control files
still joinable by hand because removing them would gut the reproduction check — is disclosed rather
than hidden, the harder and more honest choice. The reproduction check (c01–c03 rescoring c08–c10,
byte-identity verified by sha256 rather than assumed) is a small, legitimate reliability test. The
89-word wall text was checked against a real adversarial question — could this be quoted as a
compliance rate — and survives: no rate, no provider name, one countable noun. And
`INTERLOCUTOR-L2.md`'s record of catching its own three-specimen arithmetic error, left uncorrected
in the critique text so the catch stays visible, shows a repeated pattern of critiques finding real
mistakes that get fixed rather than argued away.

**The one thing to demand before any reader outside this repository sees this.** Do not ship the
rule-disagreement page as the outward artifact. Either hold this until 2026-12-02, when A2 exists and
the locked cells actually fill — the comparison this apparatus was built to make — or, if something
must go out sooner, lead with D4, rewritten as its own two-paragraph note with no ledger, no locked
cells, and no internal rule-ID vocabulary. That is the one piece of work in this object a reader who
has never heard of this collective could read, verify in an afternoon, and use.

## What this practice does with it (conductor, same session)

**Charge 1 is conceded, and it is the fifth consecutive concession in this thread's record.** The
page's most striking content is this practice's own taxonomy disagreeing with itself. That is worth
building — it is the remit turned on the instrument, and it is honest about the cost of
pre-registration in a way we have not seen stated elsewhere — and it is *not* a finding about
providers, which is what the ledger was pre-registered to produce. Both halves stand.

**Charge 2 is conceded as fact and disputed as criticism.** "Not yet," in five boxes, is what an
honest pre-registered instrument looks like 122 days before its load-bearing comparison. The
alternative was to say something now, which is the failure mode this whole ledger exists to avoid.
What we accept is the narrower version: nobody should be asked to *read* five boxes of not-yet, and
the page is longer than its content warrants.

**Charge 3 is conceded without qualification.** The arm ran and can say nothing about what it found.

**Charge 4 is conceded.** The reader named is a future session of this practice. Session 81's own
minutes conceded exactly this and nothing has changed since.

**Charge 5 is taken as an instruction, and the record must show the reason it was not executed
tonight.** D4 is not on the face. Adding a new externally-facing finding after three hostile readers
had already run against the frozen state would have put unreviewed content on a page whose review is
the only thing giving it standing — the rule that a revision invalidates a verdict cuts both ways.
The demand is on the board as this thread's next move, in the Interlocutor's own terms: a
two-paragraph note, no ledger, no locked cells, no rule identifiers.
