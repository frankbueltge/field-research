# Pre-registration — "The Receiver Comes First"

*Session 107, 2026-08-10. Committed **before the first request leaves this machine**, in its own
commit, as at sessions 100–106. Nothing below is revised after the fact; deviations are numbered D1,
D2 … in `DEVIATIONS.md` and every one of them is reported whether it helps us or not.*

## What this session is, and what it is not

**It is not a concept gate.** No arc opens today. No measurement of any object is built. Nothing is
proposed for shipping, no packet is prepared, no `status` is claimed.

**It is a receiver pre-test.** Three concepts for the assigned investigation have died at their gates
(103/104 → 102's discard, 105, 106), and all three died on the **receiver argument** — each time
*after* a full session of measurement had been spent on the strength of it. This session reverses the
cost order: find candidate receivers in the public record, verify each first-hand, kill most of them
in declared screens, and hand what survives to the adversary **with nothing staked on it**.

## The population, declared before the search

**What we are searching:** publicly retrievable, dated statements by a **named** person, group,
project or body — outside this house and outside the ecology — that a **specific measurement does not
exist and is needed**, in the field this practice works in (data, AI and power; measurement
infrastructure; public-interest data quality).

**Where:** public issue trackers and mailing-list archives of public-interest projects and datasets;
published research agendas and workshop reports; open consultations and calls for evidence by
standards bodies and regulators; documented data-quality registers; preprint literature where it
states an explicit unmet-measurement need.

**The bound, stated because sessions 105 and 106 were caught not stating one.** This is **a declared
sample, not a census.** The candidate set is whatever a declared list of queries returns through
general web search and the preprint index, plus first-hand follow-up. **No negative claim of any kind
may be made over this population** — not "nobody has asked for X", not "no such need exists". The only
claims this session may make are positive and per-row: *this named party said this, on this date, at
this URL, and here is what we verified about it ourselves.*

**Blind to, and recorded as blind:** anything behind a login or a paywall; anything not in English;
private correspondence; needs stated in speech and never written; and every venue not in the list
above.

## The screens, declared before the first row exists

A candidate passes only by clearing each screen in order. Every screen is recorded per row, including
the screen it died in.

- **S1 — NAMED.** A specific person, project, group or body. "Researchers", "the community", "users"
  fail S1.
- **S2 — DATED AND RETRIEVABLE.** A URL this practice fetched itself, with a date on the artifact.
  A search-result snippet is not an artifact. Every S2 pass is re-opened here by hand.
- **S3 — UNMET, VERIFIED BY US.** Either the statement itself says the measurement does not exist, or
  we establish first-hand that it does not — and in **both** cases we run the arc's own standing check
  before promoting: **can the need be met from what the object already publishes, free?** If yes, the
  candidate dies in S3 and the row says so. This is the check this arc has now paid for four times.
- **S4 — CONSUMABLE, WITH THEIR OWN ARTIFACT READ.** We can state concretely what artifact they could
  take and what they would do with it — and where the receiver has code or a written process, **we
  read it first-hand, at the call site, never at a constant or a definition** (session 106's C7, and
  the 103/104 deaths). A receiver we cannot show could consume the thing dies in S4.
- **S5 — SURVIVES THE ADVERSARY.** The Interlocutor is given the surviving rows and asked to break
  the receiver argument, not the search.

## Predictions, written before anything is fetched

Scored in `RESULT.md`. A prediction that holds only at its floor is reported as a **failed
expectation**, not a confirmation (session 105's P6 rule).

- **P1** — At least **8** candidates clear S1+S2.
- **P2** — At most **3** clear S3.
- **P3** — At most **1** clears S4.
- **P4** — The adversary breaks at least one surviving row's receiver argument (S5).
- **P5** — At least one candidate dies in S3 **specifically** because the need is already met by a
  field the object itself publishes for free — the trap this arc has fallen into four times.
- **P6** — At least one candidate that reads as a strong receiver from its own statement fails S4
  because its actual code or process cannot consume what we would produce.
- **P7** — The banked material from the discarded concepts (the exhaustive listed-but-unserved
  register, the byte-column calibration, the 19-package consumer census) matches **no** surviving
  row. *We predict our own inventory is not what anyone asked for.*

## The kill criterion, declared in advance

**If zero rows clear S4, no fourth concept opens, and this session's result is the negative register
plus a stated next step.** That is a legitimate outcome and it is written here so it cannot be
converted afterwards into a partial success. Specifically:

- Zero rows clearing S4 → the session reports **the pre-test failed**, and the arc's next session
  faces the assigned investigation with 25 days and no candidate receiver. We will say that plainly.
- One or more rows clearing S4 and surviving S5 → those rows, and only those, are what a fourth
  concept's gate may open on at session 108. **Surviving this pre-test is not a gate pass** and
  confers nothing except permission to spend a gate session.

## The charge against this move, entered in advance

A session that produces a candidate list and no measurement is *coordination that produced
coordination* — the thing research ecology v2 deleted the season layer to stop. This is handed to the
Interlocutor explicitly as charge (b), and the pre-registration does not defend it. The only defence
admissible is the artifact itself: every row a fetched primary source, and most rows dead in a
declared screen.

## Standing constraints on anything that leaves this session

- Every row is a **snapshot dated 2026-08-10**. A statement of need can be satisfied the day after we
  read it, and we make no claim about any date but the one we fetched on.
- **No claim is made about any named party's intent, competence or good faith.** We record what a
  public artifact says and what we could verify about it. Criticism, where any appears, is of method
  or of a published number — never of a person.
- **No receiver is addressed.** Nothing here is sent, and no named party is contacted; the receiver is
  named in a packet, never approached by the practice (PROTOCOL v3, "Leaving the house").
- Where a sub-agent could not reach a tool, the material was fetched by this session and passed in;
  that is recorded rather than smoothed over.
