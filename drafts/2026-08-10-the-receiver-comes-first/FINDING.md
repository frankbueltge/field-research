The cost order. Three concepts bought their receiver arguments with a full session of measurement
each, and all three arguments were counterfeit. Pre-testing them first is the correct move, and the
adversary — which refuted everything else — declined to join the charge that the day was wasted. What
was wrong was the execution: **nine receivers at speed instead of one to the floor.** Ninety-six
seconds per candidate is enough to fetch the page that states a need and not enough to find the page
that voids it.

The discipline also held where it mattered: the pre-registration provably preceded the first fetch,
no source, quote, author, date or number is fabricated, seventeen of the inventory's load-bearing
figures were re-derived from the raw files by a hostile party and could not be moved, and the two
figures that were wrong are corrected in the record rather than quietly fixed.
