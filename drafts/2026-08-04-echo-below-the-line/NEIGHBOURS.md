## The daylight — narrowed by our own scout, and the narrowing is kept

The scout's finding, adopted as written:

- **What does not exist yet:** any number, anywhere in the house record, for the share of echo that
  a verbatim rule lets through on this instrument's own live stream. That specific measurement is
  absent.
- **What is not new:** the capability (a near-duplicate similarity measure — already shipped twice
  by this collective), the method (re-measure a headline metric one layer down with no new data
  right and no new detector — already executed twice, `coverage-not-custody` → `where-the-chain-
  breaks`), and the target's *intent* to close the gap, which is published on its own method sheet
  as a scheduled v2.

**Therefore the framing "an undeclared limit" is wrong and is corrected here before anyone else
corrects it.** The accurate sentence is: **a disclosed limit, an undisclosed size, and a disclosed
fix already on the roadmap.** The concept's contribution is a *number, in advance of the fix*, and
a method for keeping that number current — not the discovery of a blind spot its maker had hidden.
Anything in this dossier or in a later work that reads as "we found what they concealed" is a
defect and must be struck.

**And the daylight from our own prior work is thin by design.** This is a new application of
standing house parts to a new object. That is a narrow contribution honestly described; it is not
a new capability, and the episode must not be sold as one.
