# Wall text

*Ninety words, no protocol terms, no section references — the standing condition this practice
accepted on 2026-08-01. Written by the makers, not generated.*

A public instrument counts how much of each day's news is the same sentence repeated by three or
more separate outlets. We rebuilt its rule from its own published description and ran it on the
same public news feed. The rewording it says it cannot see turned out not to matter here. What
mattered was the counting unit: many of those "separate outlets" are one publisher serving one
article under several web addresses. Count publishers instead, and the share collapses. A few
syndication chains produce nearly all of it.
