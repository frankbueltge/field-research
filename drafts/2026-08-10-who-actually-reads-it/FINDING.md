## What was withdrawn, and why it decides the gate

1. **"A researcher receives 31 % of the day's events" is false** (`CORRECTIONS.md` C5). 116,317 is a
   *different day*. The object's own index declares **178,909 bytes** for the 75 absent files;
   calibrated against 25 downloaded files of comparable declared size — median **42.0 declared bytes
   per event** — they held on the order of **4,260 events**. The complete day held roughly **40,000**;
   the client returns about **89 %**. We built the byte-column screen and wrote
   `findable_from_the_index_alone` into every row of our own register four days ago, used that column
   to *find* the day, and did not use it to *size the harm*.
2. **"A negative over 2.4 million files that no sampling gets you" is withdrawn as this finding's
   justification** (`CORRECTIONS.md` C6). Our own screen, over the index alone, puts the demonstration
   day first at **83 consecutive cycles — fourteen times the runner-up — in 8.94 seconds.** The
   exhaustive sweep is not free; **this finding did not need it.** That is the **fourth** occurrence of
   one pattern in this arc, after Q4, C4 and the unopened `gap-register-v0.1.json`.

Three more corrections followed the verdict and none of them rescues the concept: a master-list cell
that was **dead code** (C7 — the same defect that killed the last two receivers, one register level
down); a population called *"the reachable client libraries"* that was two registries of at least four
(C8 — npm and crates.io each hold a master-list-reading, non-verifying consumer); and a comment count
we could not verify (C10).

## What was measured, and stands

Dated 2026-08-10; snapshots of two registries, nineteen packages' source, and one file host.

1. **The census.** 867,935 project names screened from the Python index's own endpoint (20 hits, 2
   substring collisions); 24,719 current R packages screened over name, title and description (0 hits)
   plus 27,546 archived directories (1 hit). **19 packages' source fetched from the registries
   themselves with URL and sha256 recorded; 19 fetch paths read by hand with file-and-line citations**
   (`classification-v0.1.json`).
2. **Four executed, unmodified, against a dated absence.** On 2022-11-11 (75 of 96 quarter-hours
   listed-and-absent, re-probed by us the same day, 192 probes, and by the adversary independently):
   `gdelt` 0.1.14 and `gdelt-client` 0.2.1 each return **36,005 rows over 21 of 96 cycles** with no
   exception and **no field in the returned object marking the shortfall**; `gdelt-py` 0.1.11 returns
   **0 records and reports `complete = true, total_failed = 0`**; `pygdelt` writes **96 files of which
   75 are zero bytes and not zip archives**. Control day: 96 cycles, 116,317 rows.
3. **The adversary re-derived 36,005 and 116,317 from the raw archives rather than from the
   libraries.** The clients lose nothing and invent nothing, and the register is right.
4. **The index still promises all 75**, re-fetched 2026-08-10 (126,533,378 bytes), **1,368 days** on.
5. **The object documents none of this.** Its data page, its 2.0 announcement and one consumer's
   documentation site say nothing about a listed file that is not served, and advise no checksum
   verification. *(Three pages and one search, not an exhaustive read.)*
6. **Two of six packages that consume the series verify the published MD5**, and one returns
   incompleteness to its caller as a value. Predicted: none would. Reported as a failed prediction.

## The one defect that is real, small, and not ours to inflate

`gdelt-py` 0.1.11 holds a result container built to report partial failure and reports
`complete = true, total_failed = 0` on a request where **every file it asked for was absent**. The
adversary verified it independently. It is worth a patch. It is not an investigation, and this
practice will not dress it as one.

## What a later concept would have to do differently

Cost the finding against the object's own published fields **before** writing the sentence, not after
an adversary asks — this arc has now paid for that lesson four times, and the previous three payments
were all in the record when this session made the fourth. Classify a call path, never a constant.
Name the registries, not "the reachable libraries". And when a number makes a finding feel like harm,
treat the number as the thing most likely to be wrong.

## Standing conditions on the measurements above
