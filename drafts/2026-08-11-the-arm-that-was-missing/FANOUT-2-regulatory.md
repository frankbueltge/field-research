# Search fan-out 2 — the regulatory and institutional record

*Session 109, 2026-08-11. A search fan-out was convened because the concept's field is a legal
regime this practice does not know first-hand. **A fan-out has no vote.** What follows is its report
in condensed form, with a provenance mark on every item: **[re-opened here]** means this practice
fetched and read it itself and it may carry load; **[fan-out only]** means it was not re-opened here
and carries none.*

## 1. The legal frame

- **[fan-out only]** DSA Article 40(12), Regulation (EU) 2022/2065: *"Providers of very large online
  platforms or of very large online search engines shall give access without undue delay to data,
  including, where technically possible, to real-time data, provided that the data is publicly
  accessible in their online interface by researchers…"* — `https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32022R2065`
- **[fan-out only]** Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/2050 on data access: adopted
  2025-07-01, published OJ 2025-10-09, in force 2025-10-29 —
  `https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=OJ:L_202502050`
- **[fan-out only]** A negative the fan-out reports and this practice has **not** verified: it
  searched the delegated regulation's text for "accuracy", "completeness", "comprehensive" and
  "reliab-" and reports that no freestanding completeness or accuracy duty on the data provider
  appears in the operative text. **Not re-opened here; carries no load anywhere in this session.**

- **[fan-out only]** Commission press release IP/25/2940, 2025-12-05, reported as stating that the
  researcher-data-access strand of the TikTok proceedings *"continues"*. **This practice could not
  re-open it** — see `DEVIATIONS.md` D3. **Carries no load.**

## 4. Whether an institution asks for external evidence of coverage failure

The fan-out's answer, stated bluntly and accepted as reported: **no.** It found dated public
mechanisms for receiving *researchers' applications* (the vetting pipeline) and dated *calls for
evidence* on the shape of the rules (2023, 2024) — but **no named body stating a mechanism for
receiving external evidence of research-interface coverage failures**. This is why `CONCEPT.md` §2
records the Commission pipeline as a fact about the field and **not** as a receiver.

comes from legal-commentary sites and is **not** used here); the platform's six-monthly transparency
