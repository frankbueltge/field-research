## What this pilot is, and what it is NOT

- **It is a test of the DESIGN**, not of the question. It produces delimited units for a few files
  and an agreement figure between two counters on those files.
- **NO RATE IS COMPUTED, no unit is classified, and `downstream-commitments.md` condition 37(b)
  stays undischarged.** K4′ has fired; nothing here reverses that.
- **K4″ IS NOT SCORED ON IT.** `PREREGISTRATION-138B.md` §5's gate is *"the two counters disagree on
  more than one third of the delimited files"* over the delimited population. **Four files cannot
  score a gate over 53**, and a session that scored it here would be scoring a kill condition on a
  sample it chose the size of. The pilot reports the agreement it finds as a **count out of four**
  and nothing more.

## The two counters

Convened separately, in parallel, neither seeing the other's output, neither told the other exists.
Each is given:

- the four files' text **inline**, labelled FILE-1…FILE-4, and an instruction not to open the
  repository or run anything — so no machine count and no prior hand count can anchor either of them;
- the counting criterion of `HAND-AUDIT-137.md` §3 **verbatim**, the same text the session-138
  counter received;
- the instruction from `PREREGISTRATION-138B.md` §2 to return, per file, **the count and the verbatim
  first line of every item in the primary enumeration, in document order**, so the slices can be cut
  afterwards without further judgement;
- **no information about what the result gates, what this practice hopes for, or that a second
  counter exists.**

Neither is told that the criterion is known to be under-determined for verification reports carrying
both a checklist and a findings list (`CONDITIONS-138.md` item 3). **That is deliberate**: telling
them would seed the very disagreement the pilot exists to detect, and a counter warned about a hole
is not counting under the criterion this practice actually holds.

## What is reported, and how disagreement is handled

- **Per file: `DELIMITED`** (both counts equal **and** every delimiter line the same) · **`SPLIT-COUNT`**
  (counts differ) · **`SPLIT-BOUNDARY`** (counts equal, delimiters differ) · **`UNDELIMITABLE`** (both
  find no primary enumeration).
- **Disagreement is preserved and NOT adjudicated by this practice**, per `PREREGISTRATION-138B.md`
  §2. Both counters' reports are published unedited, in full, whatever they say.
- **The result is published either way**, including the outcome where the two counters agree on
  nothing — which would be the strongest evidence yet for K4″'s standing finding and would be
  reported as such rather than buried.
