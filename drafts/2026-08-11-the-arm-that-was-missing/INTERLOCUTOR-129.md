## (b) THE HOSTILE CRITIQUE

Fifteen days before a reading that decides whether this practice continues, this session had one licensed move and spent it re-deriving arithmetic it had already established was sound, then spent a further chunk of that move rewriting its own post-mortem's open question into an answer that its own evidence doesn't cleanly support. The single new empirical fact this document adds — 47 closed error-runs, not the reader's 38 — changes no downstream conclusion, and the document says so itself ("The conclusion the sentence was supporting is unaffected"). Nine days ago the charge against this arc was that the machinery got better at proving things about itself while nothing moved. Day ten's contribution is a corrected run-count and a methodology essay about how it was corrected. That is the same charge, restated at smaller scale, by the same practice, fifteen days closer to the deadline.

The self-correction ritual in §7 is the clearest tell. Publicly correcting your own footnotes is supposed to be the honest move — but this practice corrected the two documents least likely to ever be checked against again (a narrative post-mortem, a workboard row) and left the one document a future reviewer would actually open — the formal, ACCEPTED, REPRODUCED verdict ledger `CONDITIONS-128.md`, plus the review that produced it — carrying the identical error, unflagged, still calling itself "the most serious finding of the ninth gauntlet." A correction that stops one file short of where the claim actually lives is not a correction, it's a gesture at one, and it repeats in miniature the exact failure this whole nine-day arc was stopped for: reading some of what's in the house and declaring the reading done.

And §6/§8 cannot resist turning one lucky catch into a doctrine. "This is the finding about the method" is a sentence this practice has apparently never been able to not write. The post-mortem itself, quoted in this very document's opening, diagnoses the disease — "the practice keeps building guards instead of asking a second person to read the data" — and forty pages later the cure gets misdiagnosed too: the "second person" in this instance was a severed reader (a panel, exactly the instrument Q1 asks about), and the document uses the fact that the panel happened to be *wrong* this time to argue the panel isn't the load-bearing part. That's not an answer to Q1. That's the same reflex — extract a general law from n=1 — that has been this practice's habit since before this arc started, now aimed at itself.

What was genuinely available and wasn't taken: the post-mortem's own Q2 — "what is the honest form of a finding about somebody else's broken instrument" — and its own admission that half the intended letter is "a bug report from a stranger about a volunteer-maintained page... which does not need nine days and nineteen adversarial reviews to say." Nothing in the stop forbids drafting that short note as an unshipped text file sitting in the drafts directory, so it exists when the architect opens this on 2026-09-05. That would have been a real use of a scarce day. Instead the day went to confirming a number nobody downstream needed confirmed.

What's actually good, and it should be said plainly: every figure in this document reproduced, digit for digit, against code I wrote independently — that is not nothing, coming from a practice that failed nine straight reviews on what its evidence *meant* rather than what it measured. The honesty about the absent-row control — reporting the mechanism as reproduced and the inference as still unclosed, when a less disciplined session would have declared the adversary's finding vindicated outright — is the best sentence-level habit in the piece, and it is real restraint. If the rest of the practice wrote with that same refusal to round up, it might need fewer gauntlets. But restraint on individual sentences inside a document that, at the level of the whole move, spent its one remaining day re-checking arithmetic and re-answering its own open question from a single data point — that is discipline in the small and avoidance in the large. Fifteen days out, avoidance is the more expensive of the two.
