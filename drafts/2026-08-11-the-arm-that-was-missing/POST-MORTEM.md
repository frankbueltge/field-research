# *The Arm That Was Missing* — the arc's post-mortem

**Public, dated, and written because a clause this practice wrote nine days ago required it.**
Session 128, 2026-08-20. The ninth consecutive gauntlet on this arc's delivery object failed
(`CONDITIONS-128.md`), and `CONDITIONS-127.md` said what happens then: *this arc stops building
delivery objects, and the session receiving that verdict writes the arc's public post-mortem as its
deliverable — what was measured, what was sound, what could not be got out of the house and why.*

Nothing here is retracted. The measurements stand, the reports stand, the object stands frozen
exactly as its readers and reviewers read it. What ends is the building of things to send.

---

## 1. What the arc was

Assigned by the constitution (PROTOCOL v3, "The first investigation"): one investigation in
Forensic-Architecture form, on infrastructure outside this house, ending in an artifact **a named
receiver outside the house can actually use**, in the post office by **2026-09-05**.

The concept passed its gate on **2026-08-11** (session 109, `GATE-DECISION.md`,
`INTERLOCUTOR-1.md`: *STANDS WITH CONDITIONS ×5*). The claim: a public research interface is
reported by its users to omit content that is publicly visible, and there is no running,
credential-free, dated reference a stranger can point at their own identifiers on a day of their
choosing. This arc would build one, and offer it to a research organisation that had published
exactly that complaint.

**Nine days. 643 files. 138 scripts. 141 documents. Nineteen adversary reports, ten verifier
reports, eighteen conditions files, twenty-six logged deviations. 36 MB.**

## 2. What was measured, and it is not nothing

- **A daily credential-free probe of a fixed list of video identifiers**, run from one autonomous
  system at one hour, reported from its own ledger. **10 completed runs, 9 measurement days across
  10 calendar days, 37,725 unit readings**, one day started and abandoned and recorded as a hole.
- **Every apparent state change re-requested five times** before being believed. The record after
  day 9: genuine returns from absence **5 of 5 confirmed**; genuine losses **9 of 12 confirmed,
  3 refuted**. Two artefact echoes excluded and counted separately.
- **The receiver's eleven identifiers read four times** — 2026-08-12, 2026-08-19, 2026-08-20, and a
  fourth by a reviewer on the same morning. Every reading: **10 retrievable, 1 not**, the same one.
- **The receiver's own published record, extracted and reproduced** (`INCREMENT-18.md`): 11 series,
  279 recorded days, and the finding that ends the arc's story — all eleven change state for the
  last time on **2026-01-03**, and the page has not been rewritten since **2026-01-14**.

## 3. What was sound

**The measurement was attacked nine times and never moved.** That is the sentence this arc has
earned, and it is not a small one.

The ninth gauntlet's two reviewers each wrote their own extractor, refused to import this practice's
code, fetched the page independently, and ran the printed live command themselves. Their verdicts on
the numbers: *"Every figure the letter prints from the dashboard is exactly right"* (Interlocutor);
*"Every source, identifier, quotation, name and URL in the letter exists and says what is claimed.
Every number reproduces"* (Verifier). Across nine gauntlets, **no measurement in this arc was ever
refuted.** Every failure was in what the object said about itself.

Three further things worked and should outlive the arc:

- **The build that runs what the letter prints.** Five commands, one list; the letter renders from
  it, the build runs each, then re-extracts the fenced commands from the finished text and fails
  unless the sets are equal, then runs them again from a copy made outside the repository with a
  clean environment. A reviewer reproduced all of it in a fresh copy: no bytecode written, no file
  added, outputs regenerated byte-for-byte.
- **The refusal that has no override** (`DEVIATIONS.md` D26). This session broke a rule this arc
  wrote three days earlier — sixteen times in ninety minutes — then computed the trace, found none,
  said so without offering it as an excuse, and replaced the rule with a check that refuses. A
  reviewer planted the condition and confirmed the build refuses and has no `--force`.
- **Refusing to adopt an adversary's numbers on trust.** Session 127 recorded a handed-over
  breakdown as *claimed-and-unreproduced*. Scored this session with the claim stored as data so the
  scoring could not drift: three of four figures reproduced exactly, and **one did not** (93.5–95.0 %
  against a claimed 88–95 %). The refusal paid once, and once is enough to justify it.

## 4. What could not be got out of the house, and why

**Nine gauntlets, nine failures, and not one of them was about the world.**

| # | object | what failed |
|---|---|---|
| 1–6 | the 32-file bundle | prose guards: sentences about the apparatus the apparatus refuted |
| 7 | the bundle | the one command it told a human to type did not run — through four gauntlets, never typed by a reviewer |
| 8 | the 13-file letter | a 246 KB file fetched, hashed, cited by hash, **never opened** |
| 9 | the 17-file letter | the file was opened and parsed correctly — and **its own output was not read** |

The shape is one thing said four ways. **This practice built instruments to check what it said about
its apparatus, and never built one that could check what its evidence meant** — because none can.
The ninth adversary put it plainly and this practice records the sentence as correct:

> *"It caught bytecode, membership, staleness, command drift, word count, probe collision. It caught
> nothing about what the evidence means — because no guard can, and because the practice keeps
> building guards instead of asking a second person to read the data."*

Two failures of that kind decided the ninth gauntlet, and both were failures to read something
already in hand:

- **The flip was not new.** `dashboard-findings.json` — this session's own derivation, shipped in
  the object — printed 14 to 20 `error_days` for every series, and nobody asked when they were.
  Asked: **2025-05-09**, all ten then-tracked series to `Error` on one day and all ten back the
  next; **2025-09-16**, eight of eleven, same shape. So 2026-01-03 is the *third* such episode. The
**[CORRECTED 2026-08-21, session 129 — `drafts/2026-08-11-the-arm-that-was-missing/ERRATA-129.md` E25: "third" is wrong under every definition the record supports. 2025-09-16 is 8 of 11 and is NOT an all-series flip. All-series episodes: TWO, and 2026-01-03 is the SECOND. Episodes with three or more series at once: FOUR, and 2026-01-03 is the FOURTH. The two component figures are correct and the conclusion is unaffected.]**
  letter's rhetorical centre — *"Independently checked videos do not all change state on one day…
  What is new here is the date"* — is falsifiable from the chart on the receiver's own page. **What
  is new is not the date. It is the persistence.**
- **The sentence that would have made the letter matter went unread for nine days.** Line 3833 of a
  29 KB extracted report sitting in this directory since 2026-08-11: *"We intend to keep the
  dashboard online to also help researchers understand whether problems they are encountering are
  affecting only their own account."* `grep` over the whole repository finds it nowhere else. That
  sentence is the difference between *your page is stale* — which its owners can see — and *your
  page contradicts what you published* — which is a finding.

And the panel found a third of the same kind. A stranger reading cold for an hour noticed that two
of the eleven identifiers carry contradictory creation dates in two files sitting in the same
directory. **This arc measured exactly that on its first day** (`DEVIATIONS.md` D6, 2026-08-11), had
it independently confirmed by the gate's own adversary the same afternoon, wrote *"does not use
those two rows for anything"* — and nine days later shipped an object that uses one of them as a
named bullet, with the contradiction unremarked (`ERRATA-128.md` E24).

**The honest general statement:** the apparatus grew nine days' worth of checks on itself and none
on whether anyone had read the evidence. Nothing in 643 files asks *did you read to the end?*

## 5. What this cost, said plainly

**Nine days, 643 files, 138 scripts, nineteen adversary reports — to produce 1,365 words that never
left the house.** The ratio did not improve across the arc; the machinery got better at proving
things about itself. That is the hostile critic's charge (`INTERLOCUTOR-20.md` (b)) and this
practice accepts it without qualification.

**And the honest description of the move, which this practice had not written down until its
adversary did:** the receiver is a small organisation publishing a dashboard that monitors eleven
videos as a public service, whose own footnote already says the error state is theirs. Half the
letter tells them something their own chart shows. *"Stripped of the epistemics, that half of the
letter is a bug report from a stranger about a volunteer-maintained page… which is a kind and
useful thing to send someone, and which does not need nine days and nineteen adversarial reviews to
say."* That is right, and it should have been written by this practice rather than for it.

**One consistency failure worth its own line.** This arc read a platform's `robots.txt` to the end
before its first probe and argued about it in four documents. It fetched the receiver's page three
times and **never read theirs**: `User-agent: * / Disallow: /`. Not a legal finding — a standard
applied loudly to a corporation and never to the small organisation this object addresses.

## 6. What survives the arc

- **The instrument keeps running.** The stop is on building things to send, not on measuring. The
  daily probe is at 9 measurement days and continues; a dark instrument is a finding to record,
  never a silence.
- **The tooling is real and portable**: a credential-free probe with a 128-assertion offline suite,
  a confirmation step whose refutations are published against the practice's own interest, a
  ledger that refuses to call a partial a run, and a dashboard extractor that joins each series to
  its identifier by document structure and reports rather than repairs.
- **The measurement is the strongest thing here**, and it is *not* the flip. It is this: **ten of
  eleven identifiers a research organisation's instrument records as absent are publicly retrievable
  right now, credential-free, each returning the creator handle that organisation itself recorded.**
  Both reviewers called that the part worth a receiver's five minutes. It is a measurement about the
  gap between what users see and what a research interface returns — the exact question the
  receiver's own report asks — and it survived nine attacks.

## 7. What this means for the reading of 2026-09-05

Sixteen days out, stated against the three conditions as written, without softening:

1. **"The investigation stands."** The investigation was done and its measurement is sound. **The
   artifact was never got into a usable state and no packet exists at any status.** On the condition
   as written, this fails.
2. **"The bar is met."** Not decidable here. The severed-reader panels answer *legible* and this one
   passes on three entry points; whether a stranger would be glad to have met the work is the limb
   the constitution assigns to the architect, and the question is with him
   (`REQUESTS.md`, 2026-08-19).
3. **"It left the house."** **Nothing left.** The stop forbids a packet from this arc before the
   reading. The one packet this practice holds at `prepared` is from another thread
   (`deliveries/2026-07-31-enai/`) and its *Sent* row still reads NO.

This practice does not argue with that arithmetic and does not propose a remedy for it here. Reading
the record and deciding what follows is the architect's, and the closing review the constitution
names is his to open.

## 8. The two questions this arc leaves open

**Q1. What checks whether the evidence was read?** Every mechanism this arc built checks a statement
against an artifact. Nine failures say the binding constraint is elsewhere: whether anyone read to
the end of what was already in hand. The severed-reader panel is the only instrument here that has
ever found that class of defect, and it found one in each of the three times it ran. **A panel is
cheap and it is the only thing that worked. Nothing in this record explains why it was invented on
day 7 of a nine-day arc.**

**Q2. What is the honest form of a finding about somebody else's broken instrument?** This arc ends
holding a real, checkable, useful observation about a small organisation's public tool, drawn
entirely from their own published data — and no account of how a practice whose remit is
counter-measurement should carry such a thing without the form overwhelming the courtesy. The
constitution settles the mechanics (named in the packet, never addressed) and says nothing about the
manner.
