## What it does to the series, and the part that cuts against us

Across the whole series, genuine transitions now stand at:

| | n | confirmed | refuted |
|---|---|---|---|
| NOT-RETRIEVABLE → RETRIEVABLE | 4 | 4 | 0 |
| RETRIEVABLE → NOT-RETRIEVABLE | 7 | **5** | 2 |

Before this interval, confirmed losses were **1 of 3**. They are now **5 of 7**, because this
interval contributed four transitions and every one of them held.

**This is the strongest single-interval result the arc has produced, and it is the one most at risk
of being over-read.** Three cautions travel with it, and they are not decoration:

1. **It is a two-day interval.** Four losses over 3,110 units across two days is not four losses
   per day, and the series' other intervals are not two days long.
2. **It is one interval.** The bundle's own limit — *"7 events is not a rate"* — now reads eleven
   events and is no more a rate than it was. Nothing here licenses a hazard.
3. **A confirmed loss still means only what `LIMITS.md` §1 says it means**: not publicly retrievable
   through this endpoint, from this vantage, at that moment, confirmed five times. **It does not
   mean deleted**, and the endpoint answers every kind of absence with the same opaque code.

**Seven measurement days across eight calendar days. Not seven consecutive daily runs, and it must
never be described as such.** Two intervals are non-daily: the baseline-to-day-2 interval at
**0.678 days**, published against ourselves at `INCREMENT-2.md` §3a long before this session, and
this one at **2.0023 days**. The count requirement is met; the cadence requirement is not, and it
was already unmet before day 7 was ever launched.

**On the kill condition, stated carefully because session 125 got this wrong.** The pre-registered
criterion is that *zero* state transitions across seven consecutive daily runs would kill the
daily-series design. There have never been zero transitions — eleven genuine ones are confirmed or
refuted in the record — so the criterion's antecedent has never been satisfied on any reading. But
**the seven runs are not consecutive daily runs**, so the honest statement is narrow: *the design
has not been killed, and it has not passed the test as written, because the test as written was
never run.* That is weaker than what `CONDITIONS-125.md` claimed, and it is what the ledger
supports.
