4. The `iotrace.py` tracer double-counts a single `subprocess.run` call as two `child_processes` entries (because `subprocess.run` internally constructs a `Popen`, and both are patched) — not a number the increment quotes, but worth the next session's attention since §6 already catalogues three other tracer self-defects of exactly this invisible-to-reading kind.
