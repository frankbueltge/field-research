**Session 138, 2026-08-29.** The first execution of `PREREGISTRATION-138B.md` §2. Run under
`PREREGISTRATION-138C.md`, whose seed and four verdict categories were **written and pushed before
the draw**, and which exists because `INTERLOCUTOR-138.md`'s hostile critique — accepted without
qualification — pointed out that this session had five idle role slots and had spent none of them on
the work it says is owed.

**Draw:** seed **1381** over the **38** files that have never been hand-counted (the 53 minus v1's

## What this session verified before adopting any of it

**`INTERLOCUTOR-131.md` — v2's ten is three lists welded into one.** Its LISTNUM family matches at
lines 20–28 (the report's five reasoning items), **71 and 76** (the disposition's two errors) and
**95–99** (three binding conditions) — three enumerations in three different sections, under
`## The report, verbatim`, `## The disposition…` and `## Binding on this session`. Both counters
took the five, independently, and both named the other two lists in their REJECTED sections with
substantially the same reason. **This is a fourth failure mode**, distinct from the two K4′ found:
not chapters, not remedies, but *one delimiter family spanning sections that are not the same
document-level list.* `carve_audit_138.py` cannot see it — D2 fires only on the LABELLED family.

**`VERIFIER-131.md` — v2's five is the caveats list.** Its LISTNUM family matches only at lines
115–119, the five items under `## F — Fragile / ambiguous points`. The report's own enumeration is
its **fourteen** `**A1.**`–`**E2.**` bold labels (counted here: 14 such lines in the file). **Both
counters rejected v2's five outright**; they split on whether F's five plain-numbered caveats join
the A–E family (14) or not (19).

**The split is the criterion defect, for the third independent time.** Neither counter was told
about it. Both returned **MEDIUM** confidence on this file and no other, both named the *same*
alternative — the five-row disposition table — and both wrote out the same ambiguity in their own
words: does "the report" mean the material the document itself labels *"the report, verbatim"*, or
does it include the disposition appendix a second party added afterwards? `carve_audit_138.py`'s
validation failure on `VERIFIER-133.md` and the K4′ counter's unprompted note on `VERIFIER-125.md`
are the first two instances. **Three instruments that could not see each other have now landed on
one hole in `HAND-AUDIT-137.md` §3.**

## What the pilot establishes, and what it does not

**It establishes that the design runs and produces what it promised**: on three files of four, two
counters who could not see each other produced identical counts *and* identical delimiter lines —
which is what makes a slice mechanical afterwards — and on the fourth, the disagreement is preserved
rather than adjudicated, exactly as `PREREGISTRATION-138B.md` §2 requires. **It cost two role slots
and about six minutes.**

**It does not establish a rate, a gate, or a population figure.** Four files are four files. And it
adds two more disagreements between the machine and hand counting: **counting every file ever
hand-counted against v2 — the gate five, the K4′ five, and these four — the extractor is wrong on
five of fourteen.** *Five of fourteen is a count, not a rate*, and this arc has published against
itself that six events is not one.

**The cheapest and most useful thing in it**, for whoever runs the next session: two counters
independently produced *identical delimiter lines* on three files. The expensive part of the
replacement design — that a hand delimitation must be sliceable without further judgement — is the
part that worked first time.
