## What this does not establish

**Seven events is not a rate**, and it is now seven rather than six — which is the point. The
series has produced **four confirmed returns and one confirmed loss**, and a count of events is
not a hazard. Nothing here revises the withdrawn claim that a cross-section's age gradient can be
read forward as a rate of disappearance.

**And it immediately falsified a heading of our own.** `deliverable-v0.3/LIMITS.md` shipped a
section headed *"Six events is not a rate"* — a number typed as a word, which this session's own
prose audit is blind to and which this session published as its erratum **E3** before the
reviewers reported. With this interval the true count is **seven**. The heading in version 0.3.1
is computed from the confirmation record rather than typed, so it moves with the data; the frozen
bundle at its stated cut-off still reads *six*, correctly for the five-day panel it covers and its
four sidecars. **The defect predicted its own recurrence and the recurrence arrived the same
night.**
