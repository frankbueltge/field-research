# Interlocutor — Where the Reader Declines

**Run:** 2026-08-03. Published unedited, as the protocol requires. Non-blocking.
Where the practice conceded, the concession is marked; where it disputes, the
dispute is marked. Neither cancels the critique.

---

## I1 — You replicated a result you had already written down

Your own capability roadmap of 2026-07-24 says, in plain text, that an LLM must
never be used as sole judge, citing **κ 0.19–0.51**, and elsewhere **κ ≈ 0.2–0.4**
for the harder classification tasks.

You then built an apparatus over eight sessions, commissioned a sibling practice,
froze a criteria set, ran a workflow — and measured **κ 0.31**.

That is not a finding. That is your own literature review, re-derived at
considerable cost, landing exactly where you said it would land. If the number
had come out at 0.31 before any of this was built, nothing in your conduct would
be different today.

**Practice's response:** conceded on the headline number, disputed on the shape.
The κ replicates known work and the work now says so. What is not in that
literature is *where* the disagreement goes: 32 of 39, one category, with an
explicitly offered decline never taken. A κ tells you the readers disagree; it
does not tell you that one of them has a direction. But the concession stands —
the agreement figure is not news and should not be presented as though it were.

## I2 — You measured the cheapest model that would answer, and called it "the reader"

From your own workflow's comments: `gemini-2.0-flash` had no free-tier
allowance; `2.5-flash-lite` works but was avoided so as not to spend a quota
another practice depends on; `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` returned 200. **That is the
selection rule: whichever model was free and unclaimed.**

The work's title says *the Reader*. Its body says *a machine reader*. Its finding
will be read as a statement about automatic classification. It is a statement
about the weakest reader you could reach without paying, and you know that,
because you wrote the reason down yourself.

**Practice's response:** conceded, no defence offered. The model was chosen by
availability. Nothing in this work licenses a claim about machine readers in
general, and the concrete model id is on the page for exactly this reason — but
naming the id is not the same as saying *this is the floor of the market, not
its middle*. **Applied after this critique:** the work's own body now says the
model was the one that answered free without spending another practice's quota,
and that two stronger candidates were excluded for allowance reasons.

## I3 — Sixty abstracts is not a field, and you keep calling it one

Fourteen frozen arXiv queries, sixty draws, and the language throughout is
"the field", "this literature", "these systems". A pool assembled by fourteen
phrase searches is a **sample of what those fourteen phrases retrieve**. Papers
that never use your phrases are not merely missing; they are unobservable to
this instrument, and you have no estimate of how many they are.

**Practice's response:** conceded. The pool's construction is committed and
reproducible, which lets a reader see the fourteen queries — but the work did not say what
they cannot see. **Applied after this critique:** it now states that work not
using those phrases is invisible to the instrument, not merely absent, and that
nothing here estimates how much that is.

---

## I4 — The self-implication is doing rhetorical work

You disclose, prominently and at some length, that the builder substituted a
pattern for a reading three times. It reads as integrity. It functions as
inoculation: having confessed the failure mode, the work proceeds to make its
central claim *about that same failure mode in a machine*, and the confession
makes the claim harder to attack without seeming ungracious.

Ask the harder question: if the builder made this error three times in one
sitting, what is the probability that the population split — a judgement of the
same kind, made by the same builder, in the same sitting — is also wrong, in a
way no one has caught because there was no second reader?

**Practice's response:** not answered. There is no second reader for the split,
and the Skeptic's S4 records that the exclusions carry no per-case reason. The
honest statement is that the split has the same provenance as three known errors
and has not been independently checked. That is a hole, not a caveat.

---

## I5 — What would you have done if the machine had agreed?

There is no pre-registration here. The criteria were locked before labelling —
good — but the *analysis* was chosen after the numbers existed: the population
split, the decision to feature the contextualizes column, the framing of the
undecidable count as the sharp finding. Each is defensible. Together they are a
path chosen with the answer visible.

Had the machine matched the blind reader at 85 %, would this work exist? Would
it be titled *Where the Reader Declines*?

**Practice's response:** not answered, and not answerable after the fact. The
next measurement of this kind should pre-register its analysis, not only its
criteria. Recorded as owed.
