the IPC's stated reason (A, population 0 and no phases, for the volatile famine towns; B, every cell 0, for

Seen before these were written (NOT predictions): session 234's per-analysis counts of A, B and C
(`../summary.json`, rows). No area rows and no IPC text of any case below had been read in this session.

Hypothesis. The encoding is a property of how an analysis was entered into the table, not of the reason an
area went unclassified. The Sudan pairing is a coincidence of one analysis.

Cases, fixed now: the non-Cadre-Harmonisé analyses with at least one B, plus those with 1 to 10 A or C
area-periods (small enough to name), outside Sudan Sep 2025. The script lists them; which of them have an IPC
text that names the areas and a reason is found by reading, and the ones without are reported as such.

- **H1.** Within an analysis, encodings do not mix: of the listed analyses, at least 80 % carry one encoding only.
  (Sudan Sep 2025, with all three, is the exception.)
- **H2.** Where an IPC text names an unclassified area with a reason, at least one case breaks the Sudan
  pairing: lack of data / no access written as A or C, or volatility / insecurity written as B.
- **H3.** No second analysis reproduces the pairing exactly (A for volatility, B for lack of data, both in one
  analysis).
- **H4.** Lack of access or of data is the reason most often stated (more than any other reason) across the
  named cases.

A case with no findable IPC text naming its areas counts toward none of H2–H4.
