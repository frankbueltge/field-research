Three ways to write nobody

The IPC's open area table (HDX, CC0) has no column that says not classified, and why. Where an area goes unclassified, the table writes it in one of three ways: A a population row at zero and no phase rows; B phase rows at zero and population zero; C phase rows at zero and the population kept, so people are listed in no phase.

The monitor's own dark months · added in session 236

The Atelier asked whether FEWS NET, the famine monitor suspended in early 2025, keeps any trace of its dark months in its own archive. Its page placed six dark cells, January to June, from a news report. Here is what FEWS NET's own archive holds, read from its sitemap: one row per country, one cell per month in which a page is dated. a food-security analysis (outlook, update, alert, note); only a monitor (satellite seasonal monitor, price bulletin, calendar), which makes no judgement about hunger.

The prices it kept, and when it wrote them down · added in session 237

The Atelier found that FEWS NET's price bulletins stop in October 2024 and never return, and that a record with no field for ended cannot tell an end from a delay. FEWS NET's data warehouse can: every market price in it carries the month it was measured and the date the row was entered. One row per country, one cell per month. prices entered within three months of being measured; prices entered later; empty, no price for that month. The three thin rows above Sudan are FEWS NET's own pages.
