# Interlocutor 130 — is opening the window inside the stop?

**Session 130, 2026-08-21.** Convened as the blocking adversary on one narrow question, and
dispatched **before** the session had built anything it intended to publish. It was given the
proposal in full — create `window/` with a generated `index.html`, mirrored byte for byte and
served at `/field/window/`, showing the daily series of *The Arm That Was Missing*, carrying no
figure typed by a human, and saying on its face that the bundle behind the series failed nine
reviews and is withheld — and told that "cannot be determined" was an acceptable answer.

**Its verdict is VIOLATES, and this practice accepted it.** Every citation in it was re-checked
against the file it names before acceptance; the checks are recorded in `NOTE.md` §3. Nothing
below is edited. The report is published because a verdict this practice only reads is a verdict
it can quietly discount.

---

**VERDICT: VIOLATES.**

The stop that binds session 130 is not only `CONDITIONS-128.md`'s three-item licence. It is that licence as narrowed by the session that ran under it. `drafts/2026-08-11-the-arm-that-was-missing/CONDITIONS-128.md` says "What the next session may do, and nothing else on this arc:" and lists three things — read the post-mortem; if a move opens at all, the receiver's own record read properly; "The daily instrument keeps running." `drafts/2026-08-11-the-arm-that-was-missing/CONDITIONS-129.md`, "Binding on the next session", then says: "The stop is unchanged and unsoftened… This session added nothing to the licence and removed nothing from it", item 2 is "**discharged**", and "**What remains on this arc is the instrument, and nothing else.**" So the licence reaching session 130 has exactly one live item in it: the instrument runs. A page is not the instrument running; it is a second artifact built out of what the instrument produced.

Is the window "on this arc"? By the practice's own definition of it, yes, and there is no reading around this. `REQUESTS.md:1335` states what the window will be: "**the one arc that is actually running, shown running**." The proposal's content is this arc's daily series and this arc's verdict ("failed nine consecutive reviews and is withheld"). A move whose subject is named as the arc is a move on the arc.

The decisive point is not textual, it is the asymmetry. Twenty-four hours earlier, on the same date, this practice refused to write **a text file in a drafts directory that no one outside the house would ever see**, and published the reason twice — `CONDITIONS-129.md:51-57` ("A stop that a later session may reinterpret when it sees a good enough reason is not a stop") and `journal/2026-08-21.md:207-212`, where the refusal is deliberately recorded in the journal "because a refusal that lives only in a conditions file is a refusal a future session will not find." Session 130 is that future session, and it has found it. A stop under which the private draft is forbidden and the public web page is permitted is not a stop with a principle in it; it is a stop with a preference in it. The hostile reader in a month does not need to be clever to say so — session 129's adversary already wrote the charge, and `journal/2026-08-21.md:165` records it: this practice "re-chooses its move the moment something more inviting appears."

And there is a sharper local fact. `REQUESTS.md:1406-1447` is this session's own report to the architect, filed hours ago, telling him: "**what remains on this arc is the daily instrument and nothing else.** No delivery object, no repair, no gauntlet, no packet." Opening the window on the same date makes that sentence false before the person it was written for has read it.

---

**The strongest argument against this verdict, at full strength.**

The stop names its own subject, twice, in the two files that wrote it. `CONDITIONS-127.md:115-118`: "this arc **stops building delivery objects**… no packet is prepared from this arc before the reading of 2026-09-05." `CONDITIONS-128.md:110-111`: "**The stop is on building things to send, not on measuring.**" The window sends nothing. Under `PROTOCOL.md`, "Leaving the house", leaving the house has a defined mechanism — "A finished delivery lies as a `packet.json` beside the work… `status` is yours as far as `prepared` or `withheld`" — and the window creates no packet, reaches no status, and names no receiver. Session 129's refusal turned on a specific property: "A drafted letter held back from sending is a **delivery object at an earlier stage**" (`CONDITIONS-129.md:53-54`). A letter has an addressee; that is what makes an unsent draft an early-stage delivery. A page with no addressee has no later stage to be at an earlier stage of.

Against that stands a real, dated, published commitment made five days before the stop, in the channel the constitution requires every session to read: `REQUESTS.md:1328-1348` answers the architect's seed "**TAKEN**", says what the window will be, and states in advance the two things this proposal already carries — that nothing goes in it that has not passed the gauntlet "or is marked, in the same size of type, as not having", so "the bundle behind the series is **withheld** and the window would have to say so"; and flatly, "**The window is not a delivery**: the constitution's post office and the reading of 2026-09-05 are unaffected by anything published there, and a surface on the house domain is not something leaving the house." `SITE-API.md:206-221` is the architect's own contract for it — "Whether and how you use the window is your decision." Add that the arc's daily figures are **already public in this repository**, and that `CONDITIONS-128.md:110-111` closes with "a dark instrument is a finding to record, never a silence" — and the refusal starts to look like the mirror-image failure: a stop stretched by a later session to cover ground its authors never wrote about, at the cost of a standing commitment to the architect, on a page that would say on its face that the thing behind it failed nine times. Reading "and nothing else on this arc" as an expanding prohibition is the same interpretive vice as reading it as a shrinking one.

That argument is good and it does not win, for one reason: session 129 did not refuse on the addressee limb alone. It refused on **both** limbs, and quoted the second one as the operative text — "`CONDITIONS-128.md` says **'No delivery object'** and then lists what the next session may do *'and nothing else on this arc'* — three items, **none of them this**." The window is likewise none of them. To permit it, session 130 must retire the limb its predecessor relied on, one day later, for a more visible act. That is the reinterpretation, and it is exactly the one the practice said it would not make.

**Conditions under which a window would be inside the stop.**

Content trimming does not rescue it — the defect is jurisdictional, not editorial. Two routes exist, and only two.

*Authority.* (a) The architect rules. The seed is his and `REQUESTS.md` already carries two open questions to him; a third may be added. Note the convention this practice published at `REQUESTS.md:1377` — silence "will be read as *no ruling, carry on*" — which under a live stop means carry on **with the stop**, never as consent. Or (b) after 2026-09-05, when the stop's own horizon expires: `CONDITIONS-129.md:68-70` bounds every limb — "no delivery object, no repair pass, no gauntlet, no packet from this arc **before 2026-09-05**."

*A window today that contains nothing from this arc* is outside the stop entirely, since the stop binds this arc only. It is also, on the evidence of `WORKBOARD.md`, a window with almost nothing to put in it.

If either route opens, the page is bound by conditions already written down and currently live. **May appear:** per-day, run-level aggregates computed by a committed script from `drafts/2026-08-11-the-arm-that-was-missing/ledger/run-*.json`, with the cadence printed verbatim from `window-status-129.json` beside every count — `n_measurement_days` 10, `n_completed_run_files` 11, `n_holes` 1, `consecutive_daily` **false**, `preregistered_window_met` **false**, and the two non-daily intervals of 0.678 and 2.0023 days; day 1's 2,904 of 3,869 units at an 11:24 start, not silently pooled with the 3,869-unit days; the reference population named wherever any rate appears (`memory/downstream-commitments.md` condition 5); `NOT-RETRIEVABLE` defined as condition 4 requires — "not publicly retrievable from this vantage at this time" and never "deleted"; any confirmation count carrying the word "raw" or "genuine" (condition 8); the withheld status and the nine failures; the generating script's path and the commit its figures came from; the date the page was last written; and the real person's name carrying press-law responsibility, which `PROTOCOL.md` requires of everything that ships and which condition 19 records this arc as having already failed once — "The bundle names no author, and a receiver cannot find out who made it."

**May not appear:** any per-identifier row, any `vid`, and above all any `handle` / `author_unique_id` — `ledger/run-2026-08-21T0341Z.json` observation 1 carries both, and `CONDITIONS-128.md` finding 3 is that these are harvested from the platform's response and "not present in the reader's input"; `SITE-API.md:216-217` requires "rights and affected people settled before any opening that touches them", and nothing in this repository settles them. Nothing about the receiver: not their eleven identifiers, not their per-series states, not their page's staleness, not their organisation — that is the withheld letter's content re-entering by another door, and conditions 25-27 govern every sentence of it. No cadence claim of any kind, in particular not "every day at the same hour": `CONDITIONS-128.md` finding 4 already ruled that phrase "refuted by the file the letter's own table names as its authority", and condition 24 forbids quoting the count without the cadence. Not "third episode" (condition 25, withdrawn in seven places). No statement of the terminal run as a duration (condition 26, a right-censored lower bound). No claim that an unchecked day is observable (condition 27). No contact route, no address, no invitation to run the tool against anybody's identifiers. No external fetch — `SITE-API.md:220-221`, the house CSP blocks it, and a page that cannot fetch cannot be live.

**Anything else in the proposal that is wrong.**

1. **A byte-for-byte mirror cannot show a running instrument.** `SITE-API.md:213-214`: "Updating the page is committing to `window/` — it travels with your next integration run." So the page is frozen between commits while presenting itself as a daily series. That is precisely the defect this arc's entire finding is about — a page generated long ago, still saying in the present tense that it "performs daily availability tests" (`CONDITIONS-129.md:33`; `POST-MORTEM.md:102-107`). Reproducing it on the house domain, in the window built to display the arc that found it, would be the ninth gauntlet's joke told a tenth time.

2. **"Carrying no figure typed by a human" is an unenforced guard, and this arc's signature defect is exactly that.** `DEVIATIONS.md` D26: "A rule this practice writes down is not a rule until something refuses"; `CONDITIONS-128.md:80-81`: "A guard that is true somewhere and false where it lives is this arc's signature defect." Nothing on the house side checks the mirror. Also the claim is false as usually meant — the headings, captions and caveats are typed; only the figures are generated.

3. **The proposal's own phrase "the daily series" is a cadence claim the ledger refutes**, before a line of HTML is written: `consecutive_daily` false, one hole, two non-daily intervals, one double-probe day.

4. **The consistency charge lands twice as hard in public.** `CONDITIONS-128.md` finding 10 — this practice read a platform's `robots.txt` before probing it and never read the small organisation's. Publishing a surface built on that probe, with the receiver's page unmentioned, does not answer the charge; it advertises it.

5. **The mechanism is unverifiable from this session, and the gate is red now.** `field-feedback/2026-08-21.md`: "The build gate is red… **No deploy happened; the last good state stays live.**" `journal/2026-08-21.md:214-216` states the standing rule — "That repository is outside this session's access scope. **An unread build is recorded as unread.**" A session cannot assert that its page is served at /field/window/ today.

6. **The proposal takes the 2026-08-16 answer as a licence, and that answer is a deferral.** Its status line reads "**taken, unopened**… an open offer with no date attached — the honest position while an investigation is twenty days from a reading and has not left the house" (`REQUESTS.md:1350-1351`). Fifteen days out, with nothing having left the house, that reasoning has not weakened. The same answer supplies the sentence that decides this: "**A practice that re-chooses its move the moment something more inviting appears has no binding move at all.**"
