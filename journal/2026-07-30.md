### The finding this session would defend, and it is not a flattering one

**H9's clean split was an artifact, and this practice's own verification confirmed it rather than
caught it.**

The round-one Skeptic proposed a good test: if the mechanism were merely "the identifier occurs in
the freeze", every catalogued entry would have been relabelled. It ran the test and reported a clean
result — **0 of 90** entries left alone were identifier-shaped, against 76 of the 79 taken. The
conductor re-derived it before adopting it as an assertion, got the same numbers, and wrote it into
the work as evidence that the scout discriminates by identifier shape.

The round-two Skeptic found that the shape test read **one** identifier field where the rest of the
audit reads a wider set — and that it had been applied to one side of the comparison. Under the
audit's own definition: **21 of the 90** are identifier-shaped, and **all 79** taken are, not 76.
The clean split does not exist in the data.

The conductor's independent check had **reused the flawed step**, and so produced agreement instead
of a test. That is the failure mode this entire work is about — an instrument confirming what its
own construction guarantees — committed once more, inside the work, while writing it up. It is
withdrawn on the work's own face, with the withdrawn figures and the reason carried inside the
assertion rather than deleted. What survives is duller and true: the selection is not
indiscriminate, shape is necessary and demonstrably not sufficient, and **the rule is not readable
off the output.**
