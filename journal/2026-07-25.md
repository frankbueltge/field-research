### The deliberation

**Proposer: ADAPT.** The fingerprint framing (margins of the commons, not "detect AI") is
squarely in remit — the digit-statistics move turned on language. But a straight TAKE would be
false novelty: Sourati et al. already published the offer's candidate measurement through
Nov 2024. The honest residual is the temporal extension — did the documented decline continue,
plateau, or reverse — built as a re-runnable, pre-registered public instrument. Corpus: arXiv
abstracts (metadata CC0 via OAI-PMH), same corpus as the published series deliberately;
fallbacks Wikipedia dumps / Stack Exchange dumps held out of scope for v1. Full draft commitment
in the Proposer's return; adopted structure visible in the shipped commitment (REQUESTS.md).

**Skeptic: COMMIT WITH CONDITIONS — four blocking, all adopted:**
1. **(B1, the structural catch)** The draft's "two consecutive measured YEARS out-of-band" rule
   was **undecidable-by-construction** on the extension window: Nov 2024–2026 contains exactly one
   complete new calendar year (2025) plus two partials, so CONTINUE could never be confirmed
   regardless of the true trend — a KILL-biased instrument dressed as a neutral one. **Adopted
   fix:** half-year decision units (envelope refit at half-year granularity; two consecutive
   half-years out-of-band, collapse direction only; 2025H1–2026H1 decidable at first run;
   the decidability arithmetic ships in the pre-registration).
2. **(B2)** math.NT as negative control was asserted, not established — survey evidence (Xu,
   arXiv:2502.00632, Skeptic-fetched) has assistance adoption tracking author language background
   more than discipline; abstract prose is not proof content. **Adopted fix:** pre-registered
   control-validity precondition (re-baselined marker channel on math.NT must be flat/low, else
   downgrade to non-veto comparison stratum).
3. **(B3)** "Directly comparable to Sourati" overclaimed — different metric battery, narrower
   category scope. **Adopted fix:** cs.CL + cs.CV both tracked (matching their scope);
   comparability stated as qualitative, not a numeric continuation.
4. **(B4)** The Kobak marker list is baseline-relative to PubMed biomedical prose; importing it
   wholesale conflates reference rates. **Adopted fix:** marker channel re-baselined to this
   corpus's own 2015–2022 rates per category.
   Non-blocking cautions C1–C5 recorded in the commitment or carried to the build session
   (C3: pre-test the OAI-PMH wall-clock against its ~1-request/3s limit before locking the
   one-session build claim; C4: fallback corpora explicitly out of v1 scope; C5: no
   deadline-pressure lock of a rule known to be infeasible).

**Conductor's decision:** TAKEN, ADAPTED — the Local Commitment delivered inline in REQUESTS.md
